Explainable Subgraph Retrieval in Graph RAG

MSc research
Liverpool John Moores University · awarded with Merit

Graph RAG grounds a model's answer in a subgraph pulled from a knowledge graph. Retrieve too narrowly and the reasoning path is missing; too broadly and structural noise drives the model into hallucination. My thesis targeted that trade-off directly.

  • Built a layered pipeline combining context-aware pruning with relation-guided expansion — growing the subgraph along the relation types the question implies, rather than blindly by hop count.
  • Added dual-stage denoising, applied at retrieval and again before generation.
  • Established relation-guided two-hop retrieval as the optimal scope: enough to preserve multi-step reasoning, tight enough to cut the noise that causes hallucination.
  • Designed so every answer traces back to the subgraph that produced it — the property a deployment needs before anyone will trust generated output.
